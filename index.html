Koda Gmail

A private, owner-operated email and calendar assistant powered by OpenClaw.

What the application does

Koda Gmail helps its owner find and summarize Gmail messages and attachments, organize information, prepare or send authorized replies, and manage Google Calendar appointments and reminders.

Why Google access is requested

Gmail access supports reading and organizing messages and sending replies requested by the owner. Calendar access supports checking appointments and creating or updating events as instructed. Access is granted through Google OAuth; this website does not collect Google passwords.

Personal use

This application is maintained for its owner's personal use. It is not a public email service and does not offer public account registration. This site provides application and privacy information only; it does not display mailbox or calendar contents.
