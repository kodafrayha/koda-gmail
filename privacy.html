Privacy Policy

Koda Gmail · Updated 27 October 2026

Scope and operator

Koda Gmail is an owner-operated personal assistant using OpenClaw to access the Google accounts its owner authorizes. Contact: kodafrayha@gmail.com.

Data accessed and purposes

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, the assistant may access email addresses, message headers and bodies, labels, attachments, and calendar details including event titles, dates, locations, descriptions, and attendees. It uses this information to search, summarize and organize correspondence, prepare or send authorized messages, and manage appointments and reminders.

Processing and sharing

Relevant content may be processed by the AI providers configured in OpenClaw when carrying out assistant tasks. Results may appear in the owner's configured chat channels. Email recipients or calendar participants receive information when the owner authorizes those actions. This service is therefore not an exclusively offline processor. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google user data is not collected by this application to train generalized AI models.

Storage and retention

Original messages and events remain in Google services unless the owner requests changes. OAuth credentials are stored in the operator-controlled authentication environment. Message excerpts, downloaded attachments, task records and references may also persist in the assistant's workspace, conversation history, logs or backups. These records do not have a universal automatic deletion deadline; the owner controls their retention and deletion. Configured service providers may retain processing records under their own applicable settings and terms.

Access and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored copies. To request removal of locally retained records or discuss retained data, contact the operator at the address above. Copies in third-party services and backups may require separate removal.

Security

Google authorization is handled through OAuth rather than collecting the user's Google password. Credentials and private mailbox contents are not published on this website. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google API data

Koda Gmail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This public website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or sign-in forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor technical information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.
